### Sales Strategy: Full Price vs. Subscription

- Jacob (salesperson) should always lead with the **full price pitch**
- The subscription model is a **last-resort fallback only** — not to be offered simply because someone doesn't close on the first call, as some sales cycles require multiple calls
- Jacob makes the final determination of whether a prospect will never close on a full-price deal before offering the subscription option

### Subscription Model Structure

- **Setup fee:** Same amount as the monthly fee (suggested ~$475–$495); can be waived or customized at the salesperson's discretion
- **Monthly payments:** Begin **30 days after** the setup fee is paid; software access starts immediately
- **Pitch angle:** The setup fee covers onboarding costs; the first 30 days allow the client to get familiar with the platform before billing begins
- **Contract term:** 12-month agreement; the term clock starts 30 days after setup fee payment; 30-day advance cancellation notice required at end of term

### Technical Requirements: Dashboard & Billing

- **Payment link generation:** Jacob logs in, generates a subscription link tied to a new contact record, and sends it to the prospect
- **Contact record fields required:** Full name, business name, business address, phone, owner name, owner phone, email
- **Card management:** Two cards required on file; system must detect and reject duplicate cards; customers can update cards via a public page (new card is added, old card retained)
- **Commission tracking:** Linked to which salesperson generated and sent the link; filterable reports; configurable default rate per rep with admin override
- **Failed payment logic:** Try card 1 → try card 2 → if both fail, immediately shut off platform access and display notice
- **Admin billing dashboard:** Must show cash collected, MRR, outstanding/failed/collected payments, future payment dates, card details, and subscription status
- **Stripe webhook:** Needed to sync billing status back to the dashboard; supports refunds, cancellations, and payment date changes
- **Auto-dunning:** Automated retry logic for failed payments on the back end
- **Authentication:** Full admin dashboard to be secured with real Supabase user logins rather than a single shared password
- **Branded emails:** Custom-designed emails with company logo for new user creation, login links, and agreement delivery (Resend under consideration)
- **Scope acknowledgment:** This build is recognized as a significant lift, described as a long process but considered necessary
