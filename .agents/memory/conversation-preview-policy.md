---
name: Lead conversation preview policy
description: Safety and fidelity boundaries for internal sales email history.
---
Show appointment email history from the frozen send snapshot, never by rendering today's template or substituting current contact details. A preview may disable links and omit remote images, but must say so rather than claim a fully interactive copy.

**Why:** Staff need evidence of what was sent, while opening that evidence must not trigger tracking, expose private invitation links, or execute email HTML.

**How to apply:** Keep stored historical content unchanged; sanitize only its preview representation. Keep manual email composition and incoming-email setup outside the internal lead conversation scope.
