---
name: Campaign setup audience
description: Why lead connection setup prioritizes office owners rather than technical agencies.
---
Campaign connection guidance must prioritize a nontechnical office owner setting up their own ads. A marketing-agency handoff is secondary, not the assumed path.

**Why:** The user explained that many offices run their own advertising and explicitly wanted setup that does not depend on having a technical person.

**How to apply:** Ask where the lead form is completed, not merely where ads run. Explain external connector requirements honestly; copying a URL or running an internal test is not proof that the office's form is connected.

Welcome-screen copy should explicitly tell first-time users where to start and reassure them that setup is guided, rather than assuming they understand campaigns.

**Why:** The user enthusiastically approved a mockup introducing “Connect Your Campaigns” with step-by-step guidance from adding an offer through connecting and testing a lead form.

**How to apply:** Use plain, supportive language that includes the team. Promise guidance, not instant connections or setup without external tools.
