---
name: Reseller business boundaries
description: Confirmed selling, company commission, access, termination and external communications rules.
---

Resellers are separate external companies, not referral affiliates or internal staff. Patient Pipeline remains the seller, payment collector and service provider. Each person needs their own login; salespeople see their own prospects/customers, managers oversee their company, and the company pays its own staff.

**Why:** The user explicitly confirmed these business boundaries.

**How to apply:** Keep reseller permissions separate from broad staff roles, practice/patient access and other companies. Keep reseller follow-up external/manual, never enroll it in internal GoHighLevel messaging, booking or automation.

Companies receive 20% of actual collected lifetime purchase and subscription revenue, never setup fees. Reseller sales do not also generate affiliate/internal-rep commissions. Recurring earnings require an active subscription and relationship. Termination stops future earnings immediately, preserves already-earned unpaid balances subject to clawbacks, and never deletes financial history or resets debt.

**Why:** The user confirmed collected-money earnings, exclusivity, immediate termination and durable refund/chargeback/dispute recovery.

**How to apply:** Use effective event times, durable source evidence, cumulative reversals and negative carry-forward rather than arrival times or monthly resets. Historical financial ownership changes require explicit reasoned owner corrections.

Owner-approved standard prices precede discount enforcement: at most $1,000 off lifetime and $100/month off subscriptions. Setup pricing remains owner-controlled; independent setup discounts/waivers were not authorized. Company payment onboarding uses secure U.S. wire/W-9 intake and monthly externally executed wire recording, not Stripe Connect or claimed app transfers.

**Why:** The user confirmed the discount limits and affiliate-style external payouts, and no authoritative lifetime catalog previously existed.

**How to apply:** Never treat example agreement wording or a salesperson-entered amount as the standard price, and never infer new permission to waive setup or pay individual salespeople.
