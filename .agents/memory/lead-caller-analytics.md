---
name: Lead caller analytics interpretation
description: Why lead booking rankings separate confirmation work from acquisition conversations.
---
- Lead Follow-Up rankings use booking outcomes divided by acquisition/booking conversations, not all answered calls. Exclude confirmed/rescheduled outcomes from BOTH sides of close rate and show their work separately. **Why:** a caller assigned confirmation follow-ups would otherwise be penalized for successful retention work, or credited with duplicate bookings for appointments already made. These are activity/outcome counts, not unique-patient or attendance metrics; do not silently relabel them as conversion or attendance.
- Reuse the existing Reactivation three-conversation qualification threshold and tie-break approach rather than declaring a one-call 100% result the winner. Include owner and inactive staff so historical credit is not lost when an office changes its roster.
