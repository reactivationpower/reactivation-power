description: User's requested source-link generation, attribution, contact sessions and reusable funnel analytics.

Discussion of this feature is limited to lead/referring/contact source tracking and the admin tracking-link generator, not text or email campaign compliance or sending strategy.

**Why:** The user explicitly corrected the scope after an uploaded conversation included unrelated outreach discussion.

**How to apply:** Keep analysis focused on attribution and analytics. Treat marketing sources separately from affiliate commission ownership.

The user wants first-touch and last-touch attribution plus a chronological, expandable session history within each lead's contact profile, potentially on the far right. Each session should show page visits, clicked links, errors and bookings.

**Why:** The user wants to understand each visit and which activity drives success, rather than only the first and last source.

**How to apply:** Design a lead-linked event history with anonymous pre-submission activity association where reliably possible; do not promise complete identity tracking across devices or blocked telemetry.

The user wants a backend analytics/conversion database covering visits, CTA clicks, information submissions, scheduled calls, four-minute video views and seconds watched. The same system must support future pages, initially covering the main website Schedule a Call journey and the /strategy-call funnel.

**Why:** The user explicitly wants reusable analytics across current and future funnels, not tracking hardcoded to a single page.

**How to apply:** Separate source, campaign, page, funnel, session and conversion identifiers; preserve both existing user journeys. Define watch duration separately from playback position and do not claim second-accurate human attention.

Use explicit opt-in for this contact-linked behavioral history, and treat DNT/GPC as a refusal. Business milestone records remain authoritative independently of behavioral consent.

**Why:** Contact-linked history is more identifying than aggregate web counts; choosing conservative consent avoids silently attaching browsing to a submitted contact.

**How to apply:** Keep refusal non-blocking for forms, calendars, video and billing. Do not turn missing telemetry into invented visitors or change affiliate ownership.

Behavioral erasure and expired-history cleanup must detach behavioral attribution from business milestones without removing the milestones from reporting.

**Why:** Keeping dangling visitor IDs both retains erased attribution and can hide real collected revenue when reports join against surviving sessions.

**How to apply:** Scrub visitor/session/funnel references and touch snapshots on erasure; keep billing and confirmed outcome evidence reportable as unattributed.
