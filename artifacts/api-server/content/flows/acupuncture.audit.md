# Acupuncture Script Audit

## Baseline

- Lint: 121 errors and 14 warnings.
- Word count: 4,001 total words.
- Scope: 24 niche steps and 64 niche choices.

## Long multi-option questions

- `opening_question` combined the status question with three answer options.
- `resp_coming_back` and `resp_fully_back` stacked several possible causes.
- All three `digging_in*` screens added examples and follow-up questions to most concerns.
- `making_it_real` repeatedly asked several questions in one paragraph.
- `bridge_no` asked three versions of why the patient hesitated.
- `obj_think` combined its question with cost, timing, and value options.

## Flourishes and intensifiers

- `transition` and `transition_fully_back` used “absolutely.”
- `making_it_real` used “honestly” and dramatic good-day versus worst-day framing.
- `lapsed_commitment` used “thrilled.”
- `lapsed_felt_fine` used a garden analogy and “best reason there is.”
- `lapsed_wait_pain` used the “body whispers before it shouts” flourish.

## Sales stock phrases

- `making_it_real` pushed the patient to agree that the concern was “not OK.”
- `recommendation` used “honored” and the time-or-money disclaimer.
- `lapsed_felt_fine` used “here’s the funny thing.”
- `lapsed_wait_pain` used “stay ahead of it instead of behind it.”
- Several screens used over-validation openers such as “I completely understand.”

## Invented patient quotes or memories

- `resp_coming_back` and `resp_fully_back` claimed to remember the patient’s relief.
- `lapsed_commitment` claimed to remember the patient’s original prevention goal.
- `obj_managing_own` claimed knowledge of how much better the patient felt.
- `making_it_real` supplied imagined reactions and quoted answers for the caller.

## Token pile-ups

- `recommendation` repeated legacy `{{rec_*}}` tokens throughout one paragraph.
- `making_it_real`, all three niche objections, four `lapsed_*` screens, and both extra objections used legacy tokens.
- The flow did not have repeated `{{provider_name}}` on a screen.

## Coaching directions

- `resp_coming_back` and `resp_fully_back` ended with unparenthesized directions.
- `digging_in*` glued “When appropriate” to spoken questions.
- `making_it_real` mixed notes, sample speech, and patient reactions in single paragraphs.
- `recommendation` placed a full alternate spoken response inside a long note.
- The original objection tails mixed “Confirm and continue” with spoken questions.

## Clinical and copy vocabulary

- `bridge_no` used “evaluated.”
- `lapsed_commitment` used “periodic.”
- Several notes used “non-judgmental” or “empathy.”
- “Balance,” “wellness,” and “whole picture” appeared as broad copy claims rather than plain explanations.

## Punctuation

- `transition*`, `making_it_real`, and `obj_send_info` used colons to join directions or thoughts.
- `lapsed_busy` used a semicolon to join spoken sentences.
- `making_it_real` contained many quoted caller and patient lines inside coaching text.
- Several titles used long colon constructions instead of short location labels.

## Repeated objection tails

- `obj_came_back_stopped`, `obj_skeptical`, `obj_managing_own`, `obj_think`, and `obj_send_info` shared the same closing tail.
- Each repeated “Does that make sense?” and “Do you have any other questions or concerns?”
- The `lapsed_*` screens also repeated the same check, follow-up, permission question, and recommendation sequence.

## Digging-in chip mismatches

- Each `digging_in*` screen had ten body questions for nine concern chips.
- The medications question had no Acupuncture chip.
- The women’s health question was conditional even though its chip is always visible.
- The recap questions were long and included promises to completely eliminate a problem.

## Chips

- The existing nine chips match the rewritten questions in the same order.
- No chip label or spoken phrase change is proposed.
