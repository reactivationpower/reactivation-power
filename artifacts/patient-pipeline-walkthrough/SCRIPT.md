# Patient Pipeline — product walkthrough (16:9 approval cut)

## Director's treatment

- **Format:** 16:9, 4:00 (240 seconds). This is the long product walkthrough, not either short ad. No vertical version until the landscape cut is approved.
- **Promise:** Patient Pipeline includes two separate products: Reactivation reconnects with past patients; Lead Follow-Up guides responses to new ad inquiries. The video begins with Reactivation's upload, training, and caller workflow, then switches explicitly to campaign-aware Lead Follow-Up.
- **Identity:** Official Patient Pipeline logo; cream, navy and teal; Space Grotesk and DM Sans. Matilda, the user's selected professional American narrator, over the existing restrained instrumental music.
- **Hero:** Actual product viewports captured from the seeded demo, with video-only staging for privacy and the clean CSV upload demonstration. Visible queue and caller names are ordinary fictional patients (Bill Carter and Ann Smith), not doctors; visible phone numbers are reserved 555-01xx fictional numbers. The doctor's name in the spoken caller script identifies the practice, not the patient. The upload capture shows a fictional office CSV entering the real drop zone, the 12 actual demo-account niche values, and the genuine column-matching screen; demo/sample-file controls are not shown. The Lead Follow-Up Campaigns capture shows six different seeded demo campaigns, with full card titles made readable and the demo account chrome anonymized for this film. Other seeded identifiers and emails are anonymized. Script text remains readable in a few selected screens; don't show the entire proprietary flow.
- **Motion:** Cursor travels to genuine interface buttons, pulses once, then the full screen changes to the actual next state. Editorial labels explain steps without pretending to be app controls. Use the whole caller viewport, not a cut-out script card.
- **Arc:** Two separate products → Reactivation's existing relationship advantage → upload → train and rehearse → due list and service context → open a call → two distinct branches → niche-specific writing → connected ad-form webhook intake and Lead Follow-Up call → log and automatic scheduling → brand close.
- **Claim boundaries:** Ad forms must be connected to the appropriate campaign webhook; only valid submissions are automatically added, not all ad engagement or unconnected campaigns. No measured savings statistic, guaranteed conversion, autonomous calling, or claim that staff must manually schedule retries. Comparison to paid acquisition is a general qualitative framing, not a universal quantified result. No real or potentially active phone numbers in the finished footage. The introductory combined-price offer is omitted from this evergreen walkthrough because the products may later be sold separately.
- **Domain footer:** Show `www.PatientPipeline.app` along the bottom of every shot, with both Ps capitalized. On the closing card, center it beneath the call to action. It is printed video copy, not a claim that a domain is connected to this workspace.
