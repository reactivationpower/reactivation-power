# Reactivation Power — mobile launch film

## Director's treatment
9:16 portrait companion to the landscape film; 49 seconds. Same palette, official Patient Pipeline logo, five-scene narration and one-price launch message. The film is recomposed for a phone: typography in the upper-center safe region; simplified app artwork centered below; no tiny horizontal desktop UI. Record-to-branch, source-to-follow-up and the two-stroke offer-frame remain the motion language. The payoff is an upright cream offer card filling the central 80% of the phone rather than a narrow landscape strip.

The narration and exact copy correspond to the five-shot landscape script; source illustration is deliberately simplified, not unedited product footage. Audio and scene lengths are shared. No price number, countdown, patient data or full script passage appears.

## Pre-code review
The middle two app shots use different compositions (quoted branch vs radial campaign), and the twin-workflow scene stacks deliberately instead of repeating either. The two headline systems and card materials keep each beat legible. Important elements stay in the middle 80% of portrait height with at least 6% width inset. The last beat of each shot begins a handoff; no separate screenshot of an entire real script or an unsupported booking claim.
